# Licensing

SMF uses two open licenses, one for the framework and one for the code.

| Part of the repository | License | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework and documentation: `README.md`, `docs/`, `templates/`, and other Markdown files at the root | **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** | [LICENSE-docs](LICENSE-docs) |
| Reference specification, schemas, examples and tools: everything under `reference/`, plus `.github/` workflows | **Apache License 2.0** | [LICENSE](LICENSE) |

## What this means in practice

- **Using the framework.** You may use, adapt, translate, teach and build on SMF, including commercially, for consulting or in products, as long as you credit it. Suggested attribution: *"Semantic Management Framework (SMF) by Alexander Nykolaiszyn and SMF contributors, licensed under CC BY 4.0."*
- **Using the reference code and schemas.** You may use, modify and redistribute them under Apache-2.0, which includes a patent grant. Keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files with any redistribution.
- **Using your own Semantic Contracts.** Metric Contracts, Definition Contracts and other artifacts you write with the templates belong to you. The licenses cover the framework and templates, not your organization's content.

## The name

The licenses cover the content, not the name. "Semantic Management Framework" and "SMF" identify this project. Please don't imply endorsement or certification by the project without permission. A formal naming and trademark policy is on the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md).

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the same license as the part of the repository they change (docs under CC BY 4.0, code and schemas under Apache-2.0). See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
