## Rules of the table

- The answer is one of five: **use it**, **use the replacement**, **ask**, **flag the conflict** or **not governed**. Guessing is never an answer.
- A situation that is not listed gets **ask**, not a default.
- If two owners disagree and nobody has decided, write **flag the conflict**, name who will decide, and by when.
