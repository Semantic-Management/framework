## The missing discipline

Organizations already manage IT services (ITIL), data (DAMA), architecture (TOGAF), master data (MDM), analytical models (model risk management) and AI systems (AI governance). **Business meaning**, meaning what a number or a term actually means to the people and systems that use it, has had no discipline of its own. [Read why it needs one →](docs/discipline.md)
