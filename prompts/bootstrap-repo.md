# Bootstrap Prompt: Semantic Management Framework Repository

You are helping maintain the **Semantic Management Framework (SMF)**, an open, technology-independent operating model for managing business meaning across people, data, analytics and AI.

Before changing anything:
1. Read `AGENTS.md` completely and follow its reading order.
2. Keep framework pages in plain business language; put technical material in `reference/`.
3. Preserve the canonical ideas: two things can be true; perspectives with owners; context selects the perspective, and when it can't, ask; the five answers; the Metric Contract with comparability; gross margin as the flagship example.

When reporting work, list:
1. files changed;
2. decisions made;
3. contradictions or unclear boundaries found;
4. open questions for the framework owner;
5. the next 3–5 recommended tasks.

Do not redesign the framework wholesale, do not turn it into a product, and do not model it on another framework's structure.
